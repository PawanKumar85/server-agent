          What the agent has learned from past outages and from you. It uses all of this when it answers and when it ranks the likely root cause.

          Patterns it found

          Counted from past outages. Something counts as a pattern once it has happened at least 2 times.

            Glitch prediction model

            A model trained on the saved history to predict a glitch or failure on each channel in the next 10 minutes. It retrains every hour and is only used once it beats both chance and the best single warning sign on recent data it hasn't seen; until then the statistical forecast works alone.

            Ad-break patterns (SCTE-35)

            Learned from the last 7 days of ad-break markers on each channel's Final: when breaks usually come, how long they run, and what looks wrong.

              Facts you taught it

              Always included in its answers. Teach it here or say “remember that…” in the chat.

                Root-cause weights

                From your Yes/No answers to “Is this really the root cause?”. A weight above ×1 ranks the server higher next time it's a suspect; below ×1 ranks it lower.

                ServerSaid rightSaid wrongWeight

                Segment-age alert lines

                Each stream's own normal age of its newest video piece, learned from the last 7 days of checks (per time of day once there's enough history). It warns after 3 high checks in a row. Warnings that settle by themselves make that stream a little less sensitive; a warning followed by an outage makes it more sensitive.

                Answers you marked 👎 with the right answer. A similar question later gets your answer.

                Past outages it remembers

                Every outage that has ended. Add what fixed it, and a similar outage later comes with the fix.

                Voice alerts

                Every spoken alert is a real Indian voice saying a line written for that moment, in a mood that rises the longer a problem lasts (calm → urgent → angry → furious, relieved when fixed). Each clip is saved with its text for training a voice model later. Your 👍/👎 and how fast you react pick the voice for each mood, and the lines you like become examples for the next ones.

                Chatbot exam

                Real questions (and every answer you corrected with 👎) asked end to end and graded: did it use the right tool, does the answer say the right things, does it agree with your correction. Run it after changing the model or the tools to see whether things got better or worse. Each run asks the model about 40 questions, so it costs tokens.
